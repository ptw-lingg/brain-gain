Your role as a trainer

Overview

A trainer owns trainings. An administrator decides who must take them. Both sides meet in the job roles.

You — Trainer

  • Trainings page: create and edit the trainings you own, upload materials, write the quiz or attach a SCORM course.
  • Publish versions: the first one makes a training visible, later ones can oblige trainees to retake or confirm.
  • Trainees page: see who must take your trainings and who is due; send reminder emails.
  • You are the contact shown to trainees inside your trainings.

Administrator

  • Sees and edits all trainings, sets the owner, orders the list, exports the Training Plan PDF.
  • Manages users, job roles (which trainings a role requires) and org units.
  • Settings: pass threshold, email (SMTP), Microsoft sign-in, backups.
Trainees see a training only when (a) version 1 is published and (b) one of their job roles requires it.

The life of a training

Overview

Create

Name, schedule, content. Version 0 — invisible to trainees.

→

Draft

Every save of materials, quiz or package is staged, never live.

→

Publish v1

Silently — nobody has taken it yet. Trainees can start.

→

Edit again

Changes become a new draft; trainees keep seeing v1.

→

Publish v2…

Notify (retake / confirm by a deadline) or silently.

  • Master data is live immediately. Name, description, category, duration, owner, schedule and preview image are saved directly — they are not content.
  • Content is versioned. Materials, quiz questions and the SCORM package go through draft → publish. The version number only ever goes up.
  • Trainees are graded against the published version. Running sessions are never changed under their feet.

Content type: choose how you teach

Part 1 · Creating a training

Material & quiz

Learning materials to study (files or links, per language) plus the built-in test, graded by BrainGain against the pass threshold.

  • Fastest way to a training: a PDF and five questions.
  • Materials open in the built-in viewer (PDF, video, image) or externally.
  • Per-language variants, English as fallback.

SCORM course

An interactive course exported from an authoring tool (Articulate, Captivate, iSpring, …). It carries its own content and its own test and reports the result to BrainGain.

  • Runs inside BrainGain; progress is saved and resumable.
  • The quiz editor is disabled while a package is attached.
  • One language per package.
You can switch later: removing the package re-activates the quiz questions; attaching one hides them. Either way the change is a draft until you publish.

Quiz questions

Part 2 · Material & quiz
  • Add question, write the text per language, add options, and tick every correct answer — at least one.
  • Single choice (radio buttons) needs exactly one correct answer. Multiple choice (checkboxes) is graded as the exact set.
  • Each question collapses to a summary: type · options · correct count. Reorder with the arrows.

Writing good questions

  • One question per key point of the material; 5–12 questions is plenty.
  • Four options, distractors plausible but clearly wrong after reading.
  • Remember the pass threshold (default 80 %): with 5 questions, one mistake already fails.
  • The answer key never leaves the server — trainees only ever see which questions were wrong, if the admin enabled that.

Save stages a draft — it publishes nothing

Part 2 · Material & quiz

Save

Master data goes live at once. Content changes (materials, questions, package) are stored as a draft — a complete snapshot of what you are preparing.

Trainees

Keep seeing and being graded against the last published version. A training with version 0 stays invisible.

The Draft pill

Appears on the Trainings page while unpublished changes exist. Opening the editor shows the draft content, not the published one.

Discard draft

In the publish dialog (Versions button). Throws the draft away; the published content stands.

After a save that changed content, BrainGain offers the publish dialog right away. Skip — keep as draft is fine: finish your work over several sessions and publish once.

Part 3

Publishing

Going live, notifying trainees, managing versions.

The publish dialog

Part 3 · Publishing
  • Publish and notify — everyone who already completed the training gets an obligation with a deadline and an email. Choose what they must do:
    • Retake the training — materials and test; the new pass restarts their cycle.
    • Review and confirm the materials — no test; their cycle is unchanged.
  • Publish silently — the draft goes live as the next version; nobody is notified, nothing becomes due. Right for typos and small fixes.
  • The Note to trainees and the email preview can be edited before sending.
The first version is always published silently — nobody has taken it yet. Emails need SMTP configured by the administrator.
BrainGain for Trainers

Versions

Part 3 · Publishing

The Versions button in the editor opens the same dialog for a training without pending changes.

  • It lists the published versions with their deadlines and lets you Withdraw a notified update that was a mistake — the obligation disappears for everyone.
  • Without a draft, publishing re-announces the current content: useful to oblige a retake after a policy change without touching the material.
  • Version numbers only ever increase; withdrawing never reuses a number.
  • Every publish, withdraw and discard is written to the audit trail with your name.

SCORM courses

Importing an interactive course and choosing its completion rule.

What BrainGain accepts

Part 4 · SCORM courses

SCORM is the standard export format of e-learning authoring tools. Export your course as a SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 package — a .zip with an imsmanifest.xml — and upload it in the editor.

Accepted

  • SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 (3rd/4th edition)
  • Exactly one launchable lesson (single SCO)
  • Up to 500 MB unpacked, up to 2000 files
  • Exports from Articulate Storyline/Rise, Adobe Captivate, iSpring, and similar tools

Refused

  • No imsmanifest.xml — not a SCORM package
  • Several lessons (multi-SCO) — export as a single SCO
  • cmi5 / xAPI packages — export as SCORM instead
  • Encrypted zips, unsafe paths, symbolic links, suspiciously inflating archives
  • A launch file that is not an HTML page
A refused package shows the exact reason in the editor. Fix the export in the authoring tool and upload again.

Completion rule and options

Part 4 · SCORM courses

BrainGain decides on the server when a trainee may finish and sign. Pick the rule that matches how your course reports:

RuleUse when
When the course reports completed/passed recommendedThe course sets its status correctly (the normal case for Articulate, Captivate, iSpring exports). A reported “failed” lets the trainee start a new attempt.
When the course was finished cleanly, whatever it reportsThe course never reports completion (stays “incomplete” forever) but does terminate cleanly at the end.
After the training duration of measured activityThe course reports nothing at all. BrainGain measures the time the trainee actually spends in the content, against the training's duration.
Allow the course to contact external servers — off by default. Only enable it if the package needs it (e.g. streams video from a CDN); it lets third-party content talk to the internet.
Single language — a package has no per-language variants; use a course with its own language switch or one training per language. Accessibility inside the course is the author's responsibility. Show attempts (diagnostics) lists what each learner's attempt reported — your first stop when “nothing happens”.

What your trainees experience

Part 4 · SCORM courses

Start launches the course

No material list — the course opens directly, labelled as third-party content.

Progress is saved

Every write the course makes is stored server-side. Exit and continue later resumes; Start over discards.

Finish and sign

Once your completion rule is met, Finish and sign completion appears — the usual identity confirmation. The reported score is shown.

Versions are pinned

Publishing fixes the course version; attempts that are already running finish on the version they started with.

“Review and confirm the materials” is not available for SCORM trainings — new course content must be run, so a notified update is always a retake.

The Trainees page

Part 5 · Following up
  • One block per training you own: everyone whose job roles require it, with their status and due date.
  • Only show due / overdue narrows the list to the people who must act.
  • Email trainees sends a reminder to the due people of one training; Email everyone covers all your trainings. Subject and text are editable.
Emails need the SMTP server configured in Settings — an administrator's job. Until then the button tells you so.
BrainGain for Trainers

How trainees get your training

Part 5 · Following up

You never assign people directly. An administrator adds your training to the job roles that require it; every user with that role sees it.

  • A user's required trainings are the union of all their roles — no duplicates.
  • Users belong to org units; unit heads see their people's status on My Team and can export the Training Status Report.
  • After publishing version 1, tell your administrator which roles must take the training.
