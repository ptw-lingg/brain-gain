What BrainGain is

Overview

BrainGain manages the mandatory trainings of our company. It tells you which trainings you must take, when they are due, and records the evidence that you completed them.

  • Assigned to you. A training reaches you through one of three rules: a job role of yours requires it, it is assigned to your org unit, or it applies to everyone. Your dashboard shows the result.
  • Material → test → result. Each training has learning materials and a short test. The test is graded on the server; passing is recorded.
  • Recurring. Trainings repeat every one to five years. BrainGain computes when each one is due for you.
  • Evidence, not paperwork. Your materials confirmation and your signed test result replace paper attendance lists.

Reading the status pill

Part 1 · Getting in

Every training has a repeat cycle (for example yearly). Around the due date a window opens in which you can take it; after a grace period it counts as overdue.

PillMeaningWhat to do
Due nowYou have never passed this training, or its repeat cycle has come around.Take it now.
Due now · by dateThe window is open; the date is when the current cycle ends.Take it before the date.
OverdueThe due date and the grace period have passed.Take it immediately — your unit head sees this too.
Up to date · Next due dateYou passed; the next due date is shown. You can still open the training to review the materials.Nothing to do.
Updated · by dateThe trainer published new content and asks everyone who already passed to act on it by the date.Retake, or review and confirm — the training tells you which.
In preparationThe training exists but has no published version yet.Wait — you cannot start it.
Tip: the filter Only show due trainings hides everything green. BrainGain remembers the setting for you.

Step 1 · Study the materials

Part 2 · Taking a training

Start on a card opens the training. The first page lists its learning materials.

  • Click a material to open it. PDFs, videos and images open inside BrainGain. Web pages open in a new browser tab. Other files download and open in your installed apps.
  • Languages. Materials are offered in your language. A small flag marks a material that is only available in another language.
  • Read everything. The test is built from these materials, and you will confirm that you reviewed them all.
  • Then click Start test →. A training without a test shows Complete training → instead.

Step 2 · Confirm the materials

Part 2 · Taking a training

Before any test starts, BrainGain asks you to confirm that you are aware of all materials and have reviewed them.

  • The confirmation is recorded in the audit trail in your name — it is part of the training evidence.
  • The dialog shows the responsible trainer with an email link. Questions about the content? Ask before you confirm.
  • For a training without a test, this confirmation completes the training. That is why the dialog then also asks for your password or a Microsoft confirmation.
Only confirm what is true. If you have not reviewed a material yet, cancel, open it, and come back.

Step 3 · Take the test

Part 2 · Taking a training
  • Single choice questions use round radio buttons — exactly one answer is correct.
  • Multiple choice questions use square checkboxes — tick every correct answer. A question counts as correct only when your selection matches the full set: no missing, no extra ticks.
  • Answer every question, then click Submit test at the bottom.
The pass threshold is set by your administrator — typically 80 % of the questions. The result page tells you the exact number.

Step 4 · Sign your completion

Part 2 · Taking a training

Submitting the test is recorded in your name — like a signature on a paper attendance list. So BrainGain asks you to confirm it is really you.

  • Enter your password and click Confirm & submit, or
  • click Confirm with Microsoft for a fresh Microsoft sign-in (accounts without a password).
  • A wrong password does not lose your answers — the dialog simply asks again.
This step is what makes your completion valid evidence. Never let somebody else confirm in your name.

Step 5 · The result

Part 2 · Taking a training

Passed

Your score and the threshold are shown. The completion is stored with date and time; the card turns Up to date with the next due date.

Not passed

Failed attempts are not recorded. Click Retry test as often as you need — the training stays due until you pass. Depending on the settings, the questions you answered wrong are listed (never the correct answers).

Stuck on a question? The result page shows the trainer's contact.

When a training you passed is updated

Part 3 · Special cases

Trainers can publish new content for a training. If they decide that everyone who already passed must act on it, your card shows Updated · by date, you get an email, and the training opens with a banner.

“Please retake it (materials and test) by …”

Go through the training again like the first time: materials, confirmation, test, signature. The new passing result also restarts your regular cycle.

“Please review the materials and confirm by …”

Open the updated materials, then click Confirm updated materials → and confirm. No test. Your regular cycle is unchanged; the card returns to Up to date.

Miss the deadline and the pill turns Update overdue — treat it like an overdue training.

Interactive courses (SCORM)

Part 3 · Special cases

Some trainings are delivered as an imported interactive course instead of materials plus a test. The course is both the material and the test.

It opens straight away

Clicking Start launches the course inside BrainGain. There is no material list; the course guides you.

Progress is saved

The title line shows Saved … after each step. Exit and continue later lets you resume where you left off. Start over discards the progress.

Finish and sign

When the course reports that you are done, the button Finish and sign completion appears — the same identity confirmation as for a test.

Third-party content

The course is not BrainGain's own UI. It never needs your password — if it asks for credentials, stop and tell your trainer.

Finished the course but nothing happens? It may not report completion correctly — contact the responsible trainer.
